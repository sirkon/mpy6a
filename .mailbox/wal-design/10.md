`write`/`commit`, and uncommitted changes must never be flushed. The change is one idea worked through the whole design

and opens the two genuine gaps the prescription leaves: the forget's scope (**U25**) and the readers' frontier

## 1. The Critical restated: three facts

**Fact — the API.** `WALFile.write(buf) !isize` is superseded: the `isize` return means nothing once records are
committed as collected batches. The surface is:

```zig
pub const WALFile = struct {
    /// Writes after committed data: the record is staged into the battery,
    /// never enqueued, never flushed.
    pub fn write(self: *Self, buf: []u8) !void {}

    /// Commit collected data: the exposure frontier moves, and the flush
    /// pipeline may carry the covered areas from here on.
    pub fn commit(self: *Self) !void {}

    /// Forget uncommitted data: staging rolls back to the commit frontier
    /// (the rollback's scope is U25 below).
    pub fn forget(self: *Self) !void {}

    // read_seek, read_begin, close — unchanged (§§8–9 of the promotion).
};
```

**Fact — the forget need.** A consensus participant stages a record when it agrees to a proposed change; the quorum may
still fail it ("others were a bit too late"), and the failed record must be forgotten — the next op is written over the
old failed event's position.

**Fact — the invariant.** Uncommitted changes must never be flushed: the flush pipeline — the whole-area `O_DIRECT`
writes, sequential per file — carries only committed areas, in both modes, at every point of every path.

## 2. The model: one exposure frontier, moved by commit

The persisted control lines were always the exposure — the walk reads `data_len` and the filled marks, and everything
beyond the exposure is invisible garbage the next write overwrites. Today the exposure moves per record: §5 step 5
persists the tail line after every record's copy, and the exact-fill transition marks and enqueues immediately. The
Critical moves it per commit; everything else follows.

- **Staging — `write()`.** The §5 copy loop runs exactly as today: the gate (recounted below), the rollover zero-pad
  with the completed block's CRC into the battery, the copy into the data areas with `persist()` of the freshly written
  ranges — data first, eager, unchanged — and the terminator rule honored on every exact fill: the successor's control
  line is initialized to the zero-terminator (`data_len = 0`, filled cleared, `buffer_seq = current + 1`, `crc_running`
  carried) and persisted _before_ the copy proceeds into it. What does not happen: the filled-mark of the just-filled
  area (old step 4b) and its enqueue (old step 4c) — both deferred to commit — and the tail-line update (old step 5):
  the current area's control line is not touched at all during staging; its persisted content stays at the committed
  values. The staging state — slot, in-area offset, running CRC — is WALFile RAM registers, a second cursor beside the
  committed one.
- **Commit — `commit()`.** The sweep, in `buffer_seq` order: every area fully covered by
  `[commit frontier, staging end)` gets its control line written filled (`data_len = LBA`, filled set) and persisted,
  oldest first; then the area the staging cursor rests in gets the tail line — `data_len` = the staged end within it,
  `crc_running` = the running CRC there, filled cleared — and persists. An end exactly at an area boundary rests in the
  successor, whose persisted zero-terminator already says `data_len = 0`: no update, no persist. Then the marked areas
  are enqueued in order, the commit frontier := the staging end, the staging clears, and the idle-kick of old step 6
  fires from here. The exposure end is a record boundary by construction: a commit covers whole records — the driver
  stages one record per `write()` and resolves what it staged (whole-staging, **U25**).
- **Forget — `forget()`.** Pure RAM rollback, whole-staging (**U25**): the staging cursor is restored from the committed
  checkpoint, the staging state dropped. Nothing is persisted, nothing truncated, no file-side work: the staged bytes
  sit in the battery beyond the exposure as garbage — under PMEM, persisted garbage, invisible (every line staging
  touched either still holds its committed values or was persisted as a zero-terminator, which exposes nothing; the
  `buffer_seq` sanity check covers the stale-lap leftovers as ever); under non-PMEM, RAM garbage. The next `write()`
  stages at the commit frontier — over the failed event's bytes, exactly as prescribed.

The gate recounts the occupation: `capacity = free_buffers × LBA + (LBA − staging_offset_in_current)`, where free counts
only buffers neither queued, nor in flight, nor **staged-filled** — a staged-filled area holds data the pipeline may not
carry, so it occupies exactly as a queued one. The PMEM exact-fill rule restates over the same set: reject a staging
whose absorption would fill the last available buffer exactly with no free successor.

## 3. Crash windows under the model

- **During staging** — nothing was exposed; recovery reads the committed stream exactly, the writer continues at the
  commit frontier, and the staged bytes are invisible garbage in both modes. This is the whole point: a failed consensus
  leaves no trace on the file side, in either mode.
- **During commit** (PMEM) — the sweep persists oldest-area-first, so a crash mid-sweep exposes an area-prefix of the
  commit: the batch's records that end inside the prefix are whole and kept; the record crossing the crash line is torn
  in the exposed stream and dropped by replay ("stops at the first torn record"), or entirely absent if the sweep died
  before its first mark. The writer continues at the replay end — mid-area, possibly inside an area the partial sweep
  left filled-marked — overwriting the torn tail, and the next commit's tail-line update rewrites that area's line to
  the truth (`data_len` shrunk back, filled cleared). No new machinery: the record carries its state (`upgrade` —
  monotone, unique per `(host, index)`), the consensus re-proposes from the recovered state, and the re-staged op lands
  where the torn one died — the same state-dedup convergence the §12 chain replay leans on, applied within one file.
- **After commit, before flush** — the ordinary committed-but-unflushed tail: under PMEM the walk rebuilds the flush
  queue from the filled lines (§9 normal restart, unchanged — filled now means committed); under non-PMEM the battery
  was RAM and the tail is lost to the process crash, recovered from the quorum through the background sync — §1's
  contract unchanged, restated: **commit is not durability under non-PMEM; durability stays flush-completion, and commit
  only makes data flushable.** Under PMEM, commit is incidentally the power-durability point — the data persisted at
  staging, the exposure lines at commit, both under CLWB → SFENCE — the property §7 today grants to `write()`,
  transferred to `commit()`, still unconsumed by any consensus step.

## 4. The folds into the promoted design

- **§1** gains the pinned decision: uncommitted changes are never flushed; the flush pipeline carries only committed
  areas; commit is the point of no return — the exposure, what recovery reads, ends at the commit frontier.
- **§3** — "one `write()` call is one record" becomes "one `write()` call stages one record"; the framing, the block
  CRC, the geometry — unchanged.
- **§5** becomes the staging path of §2 above plus the commit path: the same copy loop, two exits.
- **§6** — unchanged mechanics, one restatement: the queue only ever holds committed areas.
- **§7** — the surface statement moves with the API: `write()` returning means the record is staged, nothing more;
  `commit()` returning means exposed and flushable — never a wait for the flush, never a notification: no waiters, no
  FIFOs, no per-record events, unchanged. The driver calls `commit` on pass and `forget` on fail, at the consensus
  outcome — the natural reading of the Critical's own example — and the mapping's choreography is the `consensus-driver`
  subject's business, cross-referenced by slug, not designed here.
- **§8** — the close requires an empty staging: the driver resolves every staged record (commit or forget) before close,
  and rotation defers past an unresolved decision exactly as it defers past a snapshot procedure in flight. The close's
  own pad + mark + persist + enqueue is itself a commit-shaped exposure — the final one; the empty-staging precondition
  makes it the only one left. The §12 descriptor-only closes are unchanged.
- **§9** — the walk, the scan, the selection, the positioning: mechanically unchanged; the stream they walk is the
  exposed stream, which is the committed prefix. The offset index's entries land at commit, with the blocks they name
  becoming readable. The PMEM clause "a cursor may serve `RequestLog` reads of records that have not flushed yet"
  survives with "not flushed yet" meaning committed-but-unflushed — whether a cursor may ever serve **staged** records
  is **U26**.
- **§10/§11** — unchanged: the staging and commit paths are mode-shared (the mode delta gains nothing — the exposure
  persists are the same CLWB → SFENCE under PMEM, the same no-op otherwise, just fewer of them per record); the
  migration tool scans committed streams, offline, unaware of staging.
- **§12** — the composite fans `write()` into both sides as today, and `commit()`/`forget()` fan out the same way: the
  new side keeps its ordinary discipline throughout the procedure, and the old side too — its commits during the
  procedure grow its flushed committed prefix. At OK, the abandonment drops the old side's queue _and_ its staging
  alike: the composite's unresolved records live on in the new file's staging, the old file's stream ends at its flushed
  committed prefix, and the tail crop plus the chain-replay overlap-dedup cover the seam exactly as promoted. The memcpy
  carries the whole state — both cursors included, staged garbage riding invisibly (the tmp surface's RAM lines were
  never exposure-persisted; the fixed battery's sweep persists them as-is, marks and all) — and commits after the swap
  mark lines in the fixed battery place with the fixed battery's mode barriers. The registry, the hard-stop rule, the
  elephant's fdatasync, the whole choreography — untouched.

## Open questions

- **U25 (blocking).** The forget's and the commit's scope. The Critical says "forget about the last record" and "commit
  collected data", but nothing pins whether `forget()` rolls back _all_ uncommitted staging (to the commit frontier) or
  exactly the last staged record, nor whether `commit()` commits everything staged or a subset. Proposed: both
  whole-staging — `forget()` rolls to the commit frontier, `commit()` commits all staged records, and the WALFile keeps
  no per-record mark stack. Rationale: `docs/consensus.md` decides ops strictly sequentially (one op — one quantum of
  time, the next op — the next quantum), so the failed op is always the last staged record and whole-staging _is_
  last-record; any other shape (keep R1, forget R2) is the driver committing R1 first, then forgetting — a prefix
  discipline the driver side can always arrange. Confirm or override.

- **U26 (blocking).** The readers' frontier. `read_begin()`/`read_seek()` — and the `RequestLog` service behind them —
  read the logical stream, and nothing pins whether that stream ends at the commit frontier or at the staging end.
  Proposed: committed-only — readers see exactly what recovery sees, one definition of the stream; the PMEM clause
  survives as committed-but-unflushed (the ring tail beyond the file is readable, the staging is not). Rationale: the
  background sync pulls resolved history; proposals arrive through the inbox as this thread's work; an unresolved op
  leaked into a sync answer would escape the forget and enter the quorum's picture of a host that revoked it. Confirm or
  override.
